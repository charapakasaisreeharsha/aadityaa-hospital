FAQ

Questions?
We Have
Answers.

Can't find what you're looking for? Call us anytime on 9182140264.

Is Aadityaa Hospital open 24/7?
Yes, Aadityaa Hospital is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week including public holidays. Our emergency team is always on standby to assist you.
Do you provide ambulance services?
Yes, we have ambulance services available. Call 9182140264 immediately in an emergency and our team will respond as quickly as possible.
Is the hospital wheelchair accessible?
Absolutely. The hospital features wheelchair-accessible car parking, entrance, and exit to ensure all patients can access our services comfortably.
What specialties are available?
We offer General Medicine, Emergency Care, Psychological Consultation, Surgical Services, Ambulance Services, First Aid, and affordable Healthcare Packages.
How do I book an appointment?
You can book online using our appointment form above, call/WhatsApp us at 9182140264, or simply walk in at any time. No advance booking is required for emergency cases.
Does the hospital accept insurance?
Please call us at 9182140264 to confirm which insurance plans we currently accept. Our staff will help you understand payment and coverage options available.
